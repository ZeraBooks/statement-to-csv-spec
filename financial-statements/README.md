# Financial statements, line by line

A balance sheet or income statement PDF becomes rows in five columns:
Statement, Section, Line, Year, Amount. Comparative years go in the Year
column, not side by side, so each year's totals can be checked on their own.
Amounts printed in parentheses are negative.

`sample-balance-sheet.csv` is a fictional two-year balance sheet. Each year
balances: assets 102,350 = liabilities 33,280 + equity 69,070 for 2026, and
95,620 = 36,410 + 59,210 for 2025.

The full guide, with an income statement and the cross-statement checks, is
ZeraBooks' [Financial statements to Excel](https://www.zerabooks.com/financial-statements-to-excel).
